Sources and methodology

How FightSlate decides what is on the schedule.

What "Confirmed" means

  • Confirmed: listed on an official promoter, broadcaster or sanctioned event page.
  • Announced: date or card reported but incomplete, or only reported by non-official outlets (marked "Unofficial source").
  • Postponed / Canceled: kept visible with the change so saved calendars update.
  • Rumored matchups live only in "In the works" and never in calendars or alerts.

Times and broadcasters

  • Start times are the main-card time stated by the official source, converted to your time zone. "Time TBA" means no official time yet.
  • Broadcasters marked "unverified" were not stated by an official source.
  • Every event links to the pages it was checked against, with the date checked.
